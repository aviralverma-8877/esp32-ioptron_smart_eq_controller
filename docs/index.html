How to flash

  1. Plug the board into your computer with a USB-C data cable.
  2. Click Connect & Flash and pick the USB-SERIAL CH340 port.
  3. The CH340 auto-reset circuit drops the ESP32 into its bootloader on its own — no button holding needed. Wait for it to finish and reboot.
  4. On first install, choose Erase device if offered (clears any old Bluetooth pairing / NVS).

After flashing: pair with Bluetooth device “SmartEQ-RJ9” (no PIN) and open the serial port it creates at 9600 8-N-1.

SD card — sky catalog & GoTo

Put this on a micro-SD card, insert it in the display module's slot and the controller gains an object browser: pick a target, read what it is and how to image it, then TRACK — the mount slews there and keeps tracking.

Messier objects, bright stars, planets & the Moon.

Download SD image (.img) flash with balenaEtcher / Rufus · any card ≥ 64 MB Download files (.zip) unzip onto a FAT32 card · keeps full capacity

Option A — flash the image

  1. Download smarteq-sd.img.zip (no need to unzip).
  2. Open balenaEtcher (or Rufus), choose the zip, select your SD card, and flash. Everything on the card is erased.
  3. The card shows up as a 64 MB drive named SMARTEQ; the rest of a larger card stays unused.

Option B — copy the files

  1. Format the card as FAT32.
  2. Unzip smarteq-sd-files.zip to the card's root so it contains a SMARTEQ folder.

TRACK is only enabled for objects at least 10° above the horizon and 15° away from the Sun. GoTo is disabled while a Bluetooth app is connected.

What gets written

OffsetRegion
0x10002nd-stage bootloader
0x8000partition table (huge_app, 3 MB app / no OTA)
0xe000boot_app0
0x10000application

All four are combined into smarteq-rj9-merged.bin (flash to 0x0), rebuilt on every pio run.

pio run -t upload            # from the platformio/ folder
